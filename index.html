Help

How do I open the Parent Area?

Tap the lock in the bottom-right corner of the home screen and answer the question for grown-ups. There you can choose the languages, set a daily play-time limit, reset stickers and read the privacy policy.

How do I switch between Bangla and English?

Tap a flag on the wooden sign at the top of the home screen. To hide a language from your child, switch it off in the Parent Area.

My child's time is up — how do I give more?

In the Parent Area, under Daily play time, tap Add 15 minutes for today, or change the limit.

There is no sound.

Turn the volume up with the side buttons, and check that no Bluetooth headphones or speaker are connected. The voice plays even when the device is on silent, so a child always hears the letters.

Will I lose stickers if I update the app?

No. Updates keep everything. Only deleting the app removes stickers and settings, because they are stored on the device and nowhere else.
